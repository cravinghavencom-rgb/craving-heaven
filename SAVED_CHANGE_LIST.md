# Saved Change List

Status: implemented locally; verification and live sync are in progress.

1. **Delivery-address selector:** The local header has a tappable selector with saved addresses, GPS location entry, and address labels.
2. **Bottom-centre cart:** The cart is positioned above the bottom navigation in the brand palette.
3. **Craving Points balance in cart:** The short cart view shows the signed-in balance (or prompts sign-in) alongside estimated points for the order.
4. **Safe test ordering:** Admin can enable test-only ordering for up to 10 approved Indian phone numbers. Test orders are written to separate collections, excluded from staff queues and sales, and do not affect loyalty/rewards.
5. **Free delivery:** Food subtotal of ₹300 or more automatically qualifies, including combos and mixed orders.
6. **Free-delivery reward eligibility:** The configured free-delivery reward accepts a ₹200 food subtotal and counts combos, regular items, and mixed orders.
