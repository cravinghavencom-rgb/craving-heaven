Staff guide: explain Craving Points & rewards
  • Earn points: 1 point per ₹10 on regular menu food; combos earn half-rate (1 point per ₹20). Extras and dips do not earn points.
  • When points arrive: after an online order is marked Delivered or a walk-in order is marked Completed. Points do not expire.
  • Redeem points: 100 points = ₹10 off, in 100-point steps; up to 1,000 points (₹100) per order. In a mixed order, points discount eligible regular items only; combo and extra items remain full price.
  • Use one benefit: use one offer, reward, or points redemption per order. Do not combine benefits.
  • Milestone rewards: every completed order moves the customer forward. If a reward has a spend requirement, only eligible regular menu food counts; combos and extras do not. Free-item rewards expire after 7 days.
  • Free delivery reward: redeem on a website delivery order; it cannot be applied to a walk-in order.
  • First-order gift: the first online order gets one free Classic Crunchy Burger with any paid regular-menu item. Combos or extras alone do not qualify; ₹30 delivery applies below ₹300.
